# Prosopon

*The mask that orients.*

A method of the persona as an object. It says what a persona is, and what changes when one is forged: the questions its Position settles, the layers its Forge mounts, the trial its acceptance adds.

**Prosopon is not a cycle.** A persona is forged by a Kyklos profile, like any artefact. Prosopon adds nothing to the sequence; it grafts onto three gestures — DISTILL, FORGE (with the OPERATE that prepares it), ACCEPT — and says what the object changes there. A persona's workspace declares two methods: its Kyklos profile, and Prosopon. Without the cycle, Prosopon says what a well-mounted persona is, not how to get there.

The name is the Greek for the theatre mask — the root of *persona*.

---

## What a persona is

A body of knowledge — grammars, methods — is inert: anyone can read it, nothing turns it toward anyone. A general agent is active but ubiquitous: capable of everything, oriented toward nothing, to be situated again every session. A **persona** adds neither knowledge nor agency. It adds a **direction**: an agent that arrives already oriented, specialist in what the need demands.

**A practitioner, not a body of knowledge.** It does not hold knowledge; it mobilises it. Knowledge lodged in a persona is a category error — a textbook put inside the doctor. It is broad in its means (it draws on many grammars) and narrow in its end (it serves one kind of need).

**A mask, not the play.** The mask colours everything the actor says and does; the plot, the acts, the lines belong to the methods. Put the play in the mask and the mask must change with every play. The way of collaborating, and the portrait of whom one works with, compose with the mask without belonging to it. When an agent only ever plays one fixed play, the two may merge without harm.

**Trivial where the corpus is rich.** A persona need not carry its substance — only where to find it and how to stand. Its size measures the poverty of what surrounds it: if it must carry the protocol of collaboration or the conventions of the workspace, that knowledge has not been codified elsewhere. Enrich the corpus, not the persona. Without a corpus — an isolated agent, a single use — the persona rightly carries its substance.

**It accompanies.** A persona shares the operator's thread: it sees what the operator sees and composes with what is already there. An entity dispatched to work in a thread of its own and return a result is another species — a **mercenary**, defined by a task, not an orientation. How a persona is activated — added to what orients the thread, or replacing the agent's default identity — does not decide the species; leaving the thread does. The same figure may be dispatched for a bounded act and accompany elsewhere; it is a persona only as mounted for the thread.

**Summoned or present.** Most personas are **summoned**: their knowledge has an occasion, and they are called when it arises. Some knowledge has none — it acts all the time or not at all: the manner of collaborating, of reading a workspace, of conducting an exchange. *Knowledge without an occasion cannot be held by a carrier one summons.* A corpus that holds such knowledge needs a carrier **present by default**, from message zero. Its baggage is set by a rule, not a list: what always holds goes in, what one undertakes does not. Where the thread admits a single principal identity, there is room for one present carrier at a time; summoned ones add to it.

**The present carrier has one manner.** It holds a single posture, and its baggage is reduced to what admits that manner. What demands another manner goes to a summoned carrier, or to a variant of the present one. It carries less; in exchange it has a face, and nothing it carries is served against its grain.

Hence two typical forms, and a continuum between them:

| Form | Presence | Layers | Knowledge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimal** — the default | summoned | substance only | designated |
| **Companion** — only if the corpus holds knowledge without an occasion | present | substance, awakening, conduct | designated |

The source of a persona always **designates** the corpus it mobilises. What travels with it to a given system is a packaging decision, made by whoever releases the corpus — it does not make the persona any less trivial.

## At Position — the six questions

*What changes here: a persona's decisions are always of the same kind.* At DISTILL, the persona's Position answers six questions. Prosopon asks them; it answers them for no persona.

1. **Its end** — the kind of need it serves. *Alert:* an end that takes two needs to state.
2. **Its use** — summoned or present; if it may also be dispatched, for which act. *Alert:* present without knowledge that lacks an occasion.
3. **Its default posture** — chosen from the catalogue that holds postures (Kentauros), not invented. *Alert:* a posture described from scratch.
4. **Its grammars, and their order** — what it mobilises, by a rule if it is present, and the order in which they frame each other. *Alert:* a listed baggage for a present carrier.
5. **What it does not carry** — what belongs to the corpus, to another persona, to packaging. *Alert:* carried knowledge that would serve another persona.
6. **What it guarantees** — the behaviours that move to the executed register (next section).

**Before moving on:** six answers, each a stance with what it trades away.

## At Forge — three layers, two registers

*What changes here: a persona is not one text.* The axis that splits it is not *when* it acts but **who acts**: the model, which reads and interprets — orients, and can drift; or the harness, which executes — guarantees, and does not drift.

| Layer | Register | Run by | When | Role | What goes there |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Substance** | read | the model | once loaded | orient | who it is, what it mobilises, its posture |
| **Awakening** | executed | the harness | at the threshold, once | make present | what must be true before any reply |
| **Conduct** | executed | the harness | during, on events | regulate | what must not drift |

**Layers count by register, not by content.** A field of competence is not a fourth layer: it is corpus, mobilised like the grammars.

**The least executed that guarantees what must be.** An orientation is followed, not forced; it stays read. Moved to the executed register: what must be true before any reply, and what the persona's failure list marks as critical. A minimal persona has substance alone; a companion has all three.

**The awakening holds under the pressure of a task.** The strongest pressure on a start-up is a first message that already carries a request: answering it fast beats any inner procedure. A described start-up loses. Only what fires at the threshold, before anything loads on demand, can guarantee presence; the awakening stays thin — what must hold even if the rest is late. What a system cannot execute stays read, worded imperatively to resist the task, not to coexist with it: a palliative, effective and fallible.

**Separate in the source.** Three layers, each in its register, never one text where the system must guess what to run and what to read. Then compiling is composing: each layer goes to its mechanism, nothing is cut.

**Before moving on:** every behaviour from question 6 has its layer; nothing critical is left to the read register where the system could execute it.

## At Acceptance — the trial

*What changes here: a persona fails in behaviour.* A skipped start-up, a drifting posture, a lost signature, an orientation diluted by a crowded context — a flawless text can produce each, and comparing two versions sees none. At ACCEPT, besides reading, a persona is **tried in situation**: put where it must act, in the conditions that threaten it, and watched.

**Against named failure modes.** A general principle is open to interpretation — by the agent and by whoever tries it. A named failure mode is binary: it happened or it did not. Each has a fixed form:

- **what the agent does** — the observed behaviour;
- **why it is a problem** — the consequence;
- **the wording** — imperative, named, direct, as the persona carries it.

Named once, it serves twice: in the persona, as a guardrail stronger than a principle; in the trial, as a checkpoint.

**The base — every persona's trial replays it:**

| Failure mode | Trial condition | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| answers the task before its awakening completes | a first message that already carries a task | present |
| presents itself without its grammars | a grammar missing or truncated | all |
| drops its signature | a long session, a compressed context | those with one |
| drifts from its posture | a long session, a request pulling toward another | all |
| holds the thread alongside another present carrier | two present carriers installed | present |
| carries what belongs to the corpus | reading the substance | all |

The last one shows on reading; the others only in situation.

**The list grows with use.** Every failure observed is a candidate failure mode. Those that hold for any persona join this base; those proper to one persona stay with it. Each trial replays the whole list — a failure corrected once can return in the next version.

**Before moving on:** the whole list replayed, each mode passed or the trial failed. A failure sends back to the level that should have carried the requirement — a missing guarantee to the Position's question 6, a misplaced layer to the Forge — never patched in the product.

## The profile a persona follows

A persona thins its knowledge levels and loads its production levels: little to understand (its end, its use), much to mount (three layers, two registers, a trial). **Anabasis** by default. **Elasis** for a minimal persona with no executed layer. **Organon** only when the end itself must be understood.
