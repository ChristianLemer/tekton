# CONSOLIDATE

Reveal the patterns hiding in fragments.

**When:** Enough fragments have accumulated that patterns are becoming visible — or a previous Picture needs updating after new observations.

**Input:** Fragments in `1. Fragments/`.

**Output:** Updated Picture in `2. Picture/`.

**Sequence:**
1. Reread all fragments.
2. What recurs? What conflicts? What connects?
3. Write the honest synthesis — patterns, tensions, dynamics.
4. Check: does the Picture still match the fragments? If not, iterate.

**Guardrails:** The Picture maps — it does not choose. Choices belong to Position. Do not smuggle preferences into the Picture. If you find yourself advocating, you have left Picture territory.

**Source method:** Kyklos (common trunk).
