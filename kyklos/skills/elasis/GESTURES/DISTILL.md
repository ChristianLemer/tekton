# DISTILL

Frame understanding for action.

**When:** The Picture is solid enough to act on — or the current Position has drifted from the Picture.

**Input:** Picture in `2. Picture/`.

**Output:** Updated Position in `3. Position/`.

**Sequence:**
1. What does the Picture reveal?
2. What stance does it call for?
3. What do we choose — and what do we accept as trade-off?
4. Declare the artefact's language, when those who will receive it do not read the workshop's. Elasis has no Strategy: Position is the last level before the Forge, so the language is declared here. Undeclared, it inherits the workshop's, and nothing signals the drift.
5. Write the Position.
6. Review directives — do existing FRAME, CONSTRAINTS, CHOICES, CONSIDERATIONS, CONVICTIONS, TEMPERED still hold?

**Guardrails:** Position is prescriptive. If you find yourself describing rather than choosing, you are still in Picture territory. A Position that doesn't close any doors is not a Position. Position is situated: fragments hold views under conditions; Position recognises which conditions are ours and decides.

**Source method:** Kyklos (common trunk).
