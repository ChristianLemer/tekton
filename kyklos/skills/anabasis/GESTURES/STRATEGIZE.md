# STRATEGIZE

Plan how to achieve the Position.

**When:** Position is established and the project needs a plan — orientations for a specific objective.

**Input:** Position in `3. Position/` (and its directives).

**Output:** Strategy in `3a. Strategy/`.

**Sequence:**
1. Prospect: what approaches already exist for this objective? What architectures, patterns, or precedents are known?
2. Reread Position — what do we want to achieve?
3. What are the possible approaches — including those discovered in step 1?
4. Which approach do we choose — and why?
5. Declare the artefact's language, when those who will receive it do not read the workshop's. Undeclared, it inherits the workshop's, and nothing signals the drift.
6. Write the strategy — orientations aimed at the objective.
7. Review directives — what constrains or informs the strategic choices?

**Guardrails:** Strategy is still abstract — it defines direction, not concrete moves. If you find yourself specifying repeatable patterns, you are in Tactics territory. A strategy without a clear objective is a wish.

**Source method:** Kyklos (production refinement module).
