# OPERATE

Organise production — equip the Forge.

**When:** Tactics are stable and the project needs to prepare for materialisation — tooling, format, deployment conditions.

**Input:** Tactics in `3b. Tactics/`.

**Output:** Operational setup in `3c. Operational/`.

**Sequence:**
1. Prospect: what infrastructure, services, and capabilities are already available in the environment? What can be reused rather than provisioned?
2. Reread Tactics — what practices need operational support?
3. What tools? What formats? What deployment conditions — given what is already available?
4. Write the operational setup — concrete enough for the Forge to execute.
5. If generation scripts or templates are needed, prepare them here.

**Guardrails:** Operational organises — it does not forge. The output is a production setup, not the artefact itself. If you find yourself generating the deliverable, you have entered FORGE territory. Operational equips; Forge transforms.

**Source method:** Kyklos (production refinement module).
