# HARVEST

Import relevant knowledge from another project.

**When:** A fragment, pattern, or insight from a distant project is relevant here.

**Input:** Content from another project's workshop.

**Output:** A fragment in `1. Fragments/`, attributed to its source.

**Sequence:**
1. Identify the relevant content in the source project.
2. Extract it as a fragment — the view with its conditions, rewritten for this project; not the source's case, nor its decisions.
3. Attribute the source (Genesis link or project name).
4. Let it enter the normal cycle (CONSOLIDATE, DISTILL, etc.).

**Guardrails:** HARVEST imports views, not conclusions. Another project's Position, Angles or Choices do not become fragments here by rewording — import the reasoning behind them, as views with their conditions, then let them consolidate locally. A harvested fragment should be autonomous — understandable without reading the source project.

**Source method:** Kyklos (common trunk).
