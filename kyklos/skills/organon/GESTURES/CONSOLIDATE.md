# CONSOLIDATE

Reveal the patterns hiding in fragments.

**When:** Enough fragments have accumulated that patterns are becoming visible — or a previous Sketch or Picture needs updating.

**Input:**
- *First execution:* Fragments in `1. Fragments/`.
- *Second execution:* Fragments in `1. Fragments/` and Angles in `1b. Angles/`.

**Output:**
- *First execution:* Sketch in `1a. Sketch/`.
- *Second execution:* Picture in `2. Picture/`.

**Sequence:**
1. Reread all fragments.
2. If Angles exist — reread them. Let them act as a lens.
3. What recurs? What conflicts? What connects?
4. Write the honest synthesis — patterns, tensions, dynamics.
5. Check: does the output still match the fragments (and Angles, if present)? If not, iterate.

**Guardrails:** The Sketch maps roughly — it feeds ABSTRACT. The Picture maps deeply — it feeds DISTILL. Neither chooses. Choices belong to Position. Do not smuggle preferences into consolidation. If you find yourself advocating, you have left consolidation territory.

The second execution sees more than the first — because Angles have revealed dimensions that were invisible before. This is by design. The double pass is the mechanism by which knowledge refinement works.

**Source method:** Kyklos (common trunk). In Organon, executed twice within the knowledge refinement module.
