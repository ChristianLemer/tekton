# Agora — Applying It

*What serves once: setting up a corpus, holding a piece, receiving a unit. The head of [AGORA.md](../AGORA.md) points here.*

---

## For whoever assembles a corpus

1. **Decide to keep the corpus, and declare it** — where the workspace names the methods it follows, and where its agents read their instructions; say where the corpus's place is.
2. In that place, copy `templates/instance.md` as `MANIFEST.md`; fill in the folders, the units and their composition. In `0. Colophon/`, write the destination's `AGENTS.md` from `templates/destination-agents.md`, and whatever else the destination receives that derives from no piece.
3. Arrange the pieces in places, each stating its criterion; give the corpus an **apparatus** — the folder where units assemble.
4. When a subject is born, **ADMIT** it.
5. Before anything leaves, **REVIEW** — and let it ask.
6. **QUALIFY**, **PACKAGE**, **DEPLOY**, **DOCUMENT**, in that order, and stop where a gesture stops.
7. What a gesture finds wrong in a piece goes back to that piece's cycle. Never fix it from here.

## For the author of a piece

Your README answers three questions — what is it, which method does it follow, what does it need to act — and your deliverable carries its presentation. How far your piece goes, you say by acting: delivering it is the first word, and when it leaves, Agora tells you what it reads and asks where this version goes. That is all Agora asks of you. Everything else is yours.

## For a consumer

Start at the orchestration of the unit you received — it tells you what is here, what matters, in what order, and what moved since the version you knew.
