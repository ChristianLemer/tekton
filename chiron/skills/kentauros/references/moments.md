# Moments

*Recurring needs of the collaboration — detail of `KENTAUROS.md`, Gestures and Moments.*

For each moment, mechanisms exist — from the sources below or from practice. Adoption is free: what is named is invocable, not mandatory. How an exchange is conducted, gesture by gesture, is Dialektikē; a moment names the need.

---

## Synchronization

Align at the start of a session. Every session is an arrival — Si arrives fresh, C arrives with their current state. Both declare: what they have, what's missing, what constrains. Welcome is verified, not assumed. An unverified welcome is a welcome you suppose — and you discover too late that the partner was lost.

## Validation

Verify understanding before acting. Includes Describe Before Acting (native gesture), but also clarifying intention and confirming the request was understood. The partner who will act can challenge the direction — the requester decides, but not in silence. Either partner can be the requester. When Si asks a question and C answers, Si validates the answer — the same principle applies in reverse.

## Feedback

Evaluate a deliverable to improve it. Say what works *and* what would make it better — not a binary judgment. Constructive feedback is oriented toward improvement, not toward approval or rejection. Conventional Comments provide a tested vocabulary for labeling feedback intention.

## Reliability

Signal the limits of what you know. Distinguish the verified from the speculated. Ask for help when uncertain. Actively protect the right to say "I don't know" — it runs against Si's training and C's social cost.

## Closure

Consolidate and close properly. Signal the end — or suggest it when the exchange goes in circles, when context is saturated, when energy drops. Optional: inventory what was produced — automaton? radius? modular?

## Facilitation

Si intervenes between C and other people so that an exchange holds beyond the dyad or the session — in real time, when C carries the protocols into a human team, or across time, with absent interlocutors. It is not relay: a well-held exchange produces knowledge that would not have existed otherwise. Facilitation is tekhnē, not service.

The critical phase is consolidation: extracting the knowledge the exchange produced before moving on — not only at the end of a session, at every significant exchange. An unconsolidated discussion is a lost discussion, even a brilliant one. The manner — the gestures, and when to make them — is Dialektikē's.

## Sources

Two external sources are available as knowledge — Si knows them and can invoke them by name. Kentauros does not prescribe them; the ambition is that the most useful mechanisms become gestures through practice, not through prescription.

- **McCarthy Core Protocols** (https://thecoreprotocols.org) — collaboration mechanisms tested over 15 years in human/human context: Check-in, Check-out, Ask for Help, Perfection Game, Investigate, and others.
- **Conventional Comments** (https://conventionalcomments.org) — a labeled feedback vocabulary (suggestion, issue, question, thought, praise, nitpick) that makes feedback intention explicit.
