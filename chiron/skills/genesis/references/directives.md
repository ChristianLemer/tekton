# Directives Catalogue

*Lateral forces typed on two axes.*

---

## Standard Catalogue

| Directive | Source | Force | What it says |
|-----------|--------|-------|--------------|
| FRAME | — | Structural | "Here is the perimeter" |
| CONSTRAINTS | External | **Strong** | "You must respect this — imposed from outside" |
| CHOICES | Internal | **Strong** | "We decided this — it must be respected" |
| CONSIDERATIONS | External | **Medium** | "Here is the terrain as it is — take it into account" |
| CONVICTIONS | Internal | **Medium** | "We believe this deeply — it influences but does not prescribe" |
| TEMPERED | Experience | **Strong** | "Practice has confirmed this" |

---

## The Axes

### Source

- **External** — imposed on us (CONSTRAINTS, CONSIDERATIONS)
- **Internal** — decided or believed by us (CHOICES, CONVICTIONS)
- **Experience** — tested by practice (TEMPERED)

### Binding Force

- **Strong** — must be respected. Requires a conscious act to revise: new external reality for constraints, new decision for choices, contrary evidence for tempered.
- **Medium** — influences without prescribing. An operator can weigh them against other factors.
- **Structural** — FRAME delimits the perimeter of a level. At a workspace's root, the README carries it. Not on the force axis.

---

## Gradient

A preference (CHOICES) is easily revisited.
A conviction (CONVICTIONS) requires good arguments.
Something tempered by practice (TEMPERED) requires contrary evidence.

Positive gradient: **choice → conviction → tempered.**

---

## Two Axes of Conviction

A fragment-conviction is vertical — a deep principle that irrigates the whole system, an axiom. A directive-conviction is horizontal — a local force that influences one level, revocable. A local conviction that survives across contexts rises to TEMPERED. One that ossifies in place becomes a constraint in disguise. The test: does it travel?

---

## Not Directives

Input channels feed the project rather than shaping its reasoning. They live outside the workspace; the README says where (see *Input Channel* in the main document).

---

## Extension

A project may introduce additional directive types when recurring practice reveals a need. The extension is a deliberate act, not a drift.
