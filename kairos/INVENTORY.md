## One skill, not five

The deliverable is one method; it becomes one skill. Its lateral documents travel as `references/` — the pattern the target accepts, verified on installed plugins.

**What an earlier hand-built package carried and this one does not:** three skills extracted from the gestures (capture, allocate, weekly-review) and a fourth composing all nine. They had no source in the deliverable, and their stated justification — dedicated slash-commands — described an interface the package never provided. Naming a gesture reaches it; that is what the vocabulary is for.

*If fine-grained selection turns out to matter in use, it comes back from the deliverable — as forged frequent gestures, not as extracts.*

## The persona

**The guardian is not optional for this method.** Kairos is *an attention allocation system held by a guardian* — six of its nine gestures are guardian-initiated, and the doctrine is explicit that waiting to be asked for those is asking the human to hold what the system exists to hold. A package without the persona would ship the state and drop what keeps it alive.

`GUARDIAN.md` is the source, and it lives beside the deliverable rather than in a persona family: this guardian holds *this* system, and holds nothing else.

**Declared transformation, and no other:** the workshop's provenance block does not travel, and a frontmatter is added for the target. Register derives; substance does not — a sentence the target needs and the deliverable lacks goes up, not in.

**Debt:** the transformation is not tooled, so nothing replays it. Verified once is not derived.

## Compatibility

An existing workspace declares Kairos in its own `CLAUDE.md` or `README.md`, and the opt-in contract names what it may rely on: the nine gestures reachable by name, the state in `KAIROS/SETUP.md`, the subagent dispatchable. **All three still hold.**

What such a declaration must not promise: slash-commands, and the three extracted skills by name. Neither exists.
