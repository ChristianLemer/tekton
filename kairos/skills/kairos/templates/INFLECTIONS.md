# INFLECTIONS

*What bends the allocation beyond the balance.*

> [!info] Read by the guardian alongside BALANCE.md when allocating.
> The balance says *how much* per partition. Inflections say *what's particular* right now.

---

## Regular

> [!tip] Personal allocation rules, stable. Set at setup, rarely adjusted.
> These are preferences that don't need to be re-declared at each allocation.

- 

## One-time

> [!tip] Inflections of the moment, dated. The human declares, the guardian records.
> Expired one-time inflections are cleaned by the guardian.
> If a one-time inflection recurs, it's perhaps a regular one in disguise — or a calibration to make.
