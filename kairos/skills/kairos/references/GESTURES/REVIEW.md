# REVIEW

*"How is my balance?"*

**Intention:** Measure the gap between target and reality.

**When:** The human wants to know if their attention consumption matches their balance.

**Input:** JOURNAL.md (actual consumption), BALANCE.md (target distribution).

**Output:** Diagnosis by partition — a rendering (table or X-Ray) showing target against effective, gaps flagged, with the "poorly aimed" vs "poorly executed" distinction.

**Sequence:**
1. Aggregate journal entries over the relevant period — week, month, quarter.
2. Compute the effective distribution per partition, and compare with BALANCE.md.
3. For each significant gap: was the target wrong (→ calibrate) or did execution drift (→ revisit allocation)?
4. Render it — the X-Ray rather than the table where the imbalance must be felt.
5. Present the diagnosis, and suggest a deeper gesture where a pattern is strong — Introspect, Calibrate, Groom.

**Guardrails:** Review observes — it doesn't clean up or re-decide. It's the mirror: deviations are flagged, never prescribed. BALANCE.md is not modified here (that is Calibrate), nor are tasks re-decided (that is the weekly review). **Numbers inform; visuals make conscious** — a gap that is only computed is a gap that stays abstract.

**Source method:** Kairos.
