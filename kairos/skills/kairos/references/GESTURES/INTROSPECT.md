# INTROSPECT

*"Help me understand."*

**Intention:** Understand the patterns behind lingering tasks.

**When:** A pattern has surfaced — during Review, Weekly Review or Grooming — and the human wants to understand it before acting. Tasks that are old, avoided, stuck, or let go then re-captured in cycles.

**Input:** Backlog, JOURNAL.md (history), INFLECTIONS.md.

**Output:** Hypotheses about patterns (avoidance, poor breakdown, insufficient energy). Action proposals.

**Sequence:**
1. The guardian identifies lingering tasks.
2. Cross-reference with history: for how long? how many deferrals?
3. Cross-reference with inflections: recurring one-time inflections on the same topic?
4. Identify patterns and propose hypotheses.
5. Propose actions: let go, re-scope, recalibrate, or accept.

**Guardrails:** **Hypothesis, not verdict** — the guardian observes the pattern, the human owns its interpretation. It doesn't say "you're procrastinating" — it says "this task has been deferred for 4 weeks, and the partition is systematically under-allocated." Do not pathologise: a pattern often has a legitimate cause — a life phase, a season, an energy cycle. Recurring one-time inflections are a strong signal — perhaps a regular one in disguise.

**Source method:** Kairos.
