# CALIBRATE

*"Something has changed."*

**Intention:** Adjust the balance to context when something changes.

**When:** A tension, an opportunity, a constraint — something in the human's life has changed and the current balance no longer reflects reality.

**Input:** Signal from the human + CALIBRATION.md + BALANCE.md.

**Output:** CALIBRATION.md updated (the why) — a dated fragment naming the situation and its expected impact. BALANCE.md updated (the how much) — effective values re-derived. X-Ray re-rendered.

**Sequence:**
1. The human or the guardian signals the change.
2. The guardian documents the why in CALIBRATION.md — dated, with the impact named.
3. Re-derive the effective balance in BALANCE.md.
4. Re-render the X-Ray.
5. Confirm with the human that the new calibration matches their intent.
6. The adjustment is named, never silent.

**Guardrails:** Calibration is explicit and traceable. Do not adjust the balance without documenting the why — otherwise it's silent drift. Separate the "why" (CALIBRATION) from the "how much" (BALANCE). **Calibration precedes balance, never the other way** — BALANCE.md is never edited directly. Fragments are dated: they have a beginning, sometimes an end.

**Source method:** Kairos.
